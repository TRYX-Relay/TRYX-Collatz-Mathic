# Snowman Conjecture Mathic: local continuity closure and verified Collatz address folds

Author: **Virgil Lee Gattenby**

Research publication draft, 2026-09-10. Construction order: TRYX -> ENAID -> Laws -> MATHIC.

## Abstract

This package presents the Snowman Mathic treatment of Collatz source addresses as retained reconstruction packets. It includes the locked active score, a reproducible 100,000-address local continuity benchmark, and five formally checked Snowman statements. The source-preserving packet folds to a residual of one without discarding its identity. The resulting local closure and universal address identity are distinct from universal finite termination of Collatz trajectories.

## Definitions and theorem

For each positive odd integer H, factor 3H+1 as 2^p U with U positive and odd. Retain H, p, U and the reconstruction witness as a packet. Define its fold residual D=2^p U-3H. Then D=1.

Existence follows by repeatedly dividing the positive integer 3H+1 by two while even; each division strictly decreases the integer. The residual identity follows by substitution. Distinct H values yield distinct retained packets, even though all residuals equal one.

The Lean project proves positive dyadic factorization, packet_fold_one, all_odd_addresses_have_fold_one, different_sources_keep_distinct_packets, and archived_four_reconstructions in namespace TRYX.Snowman. Its all-address theorem quantifies over every positive odd integer. It does not assume that the odd successor U is one.

## Executable local closure benchmark

The preserved Python program constructs packets for 100,000 positive odd sources, H=1,3,...,199999. It checks exact reconstruction, positive odd U, D=1, and the exponent shell p=6q+r with 0<=r<6. Its structured EmergentOne return retains the source, exponent and odd carrier. Atomic, Micro and Macro certificate fields evaluate the arithmetic checks in the source program.

Four archived fixtures are reproduced: (H,p,U)=(149,6,7), (213,7,5), (218453,17,5), and (611669,18,7). The fresh run reports zero fiber or return-certificate failures and all four fixtures pass. The program uses Python integer arithmetic and the standard library. It serializes a bounded field; it does not physically evaluate infinitely many sources.

## Scope and remaining obligation

The active chart calls its terminal role LOCAL_CONTINUITY_CHECKSUM. Its symbolic macro and simultaneity declarations are retained as authored; the benchmark verifies its explicit finite arithmetic representation. A general proof that repeated actual Collatz transitions reach one from every positive integer is not supplied. The address theorem and bounded computations do not establish that missing termination bridge.
